Frequently asked questions

I bought full access, but it's locked on my new phone.

Open More › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to the device.

Does the purchase cover other states?

Yes. Full access is a one-time purchase for the whole app: New York now, and every state added later at no extra cost. There's no subscription.

Can my family use my purchase?

Yes, with Family Sharing turned on in your Apple Account, up to five family members can use full access.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Turn on More › Sync with iCloud on both devices and sign in with the same Apple Account. Progress syncs through your private iCloud.

How do I change the app or question language?

Open More. App language changes menus and buttons; Content language changes the questions. You can also long-press any question to switch it to English.

How do I start over?

Open More › Reset progress. This deletes your answers, review schedule and exams on all your devices. Your settings and purchase stay.

I found a mistake in a question.

Thank you! Email us the question text or a screenshot and we'll fix it in the next update.
