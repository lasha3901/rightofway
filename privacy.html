Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

In short: Right of Way doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your study progress belongs to you and stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What stays on your device

The app saves your answers, review schedule, bookmarks, practice exam results and settings (such as your language and state) on your device so it can track your progress. This information never leaves your device unless you turn on iCloud sync.

iCloud sync

If you turn on Sync with iCloud, the same study progress is stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so it's available on all your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is protected by Apple and is not accessible to us. You can turn sync off at any time in More, and manage or delete the data in your device's iCloud settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, email, payment details or any other personal information. The app only learns from Apple whether full access has been purchased, so it can unlock it.

Read aloud

Reading English terms aloud uses the speech voice built into your device. No text or audio is sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal data or require an account.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking, and we don't use third-party SDKs that do.
  • We don't sell or share any data with anyone.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app, and we don't share it.

Deleting your data

Use More › Reset progress to delete your study progress on all your devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device; data in iCloud can be deleted in your device's iCloud storage settings.

Children

The app is meant for people preparing for a learner permit test. Because it doesn't collect personal data from anyone, it doesn't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: rightofway.app@gmail.com
